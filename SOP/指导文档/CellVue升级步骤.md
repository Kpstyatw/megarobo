# CellVue升级步骤

1.  将C:\Program Files下的CellVue文件夹复制一份，备份
    
    ![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jD79QGDO01/img/37b7c9a6-9f53-4e86-b358-5934cc026b1b.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_1125,h_332/ignore-error,1)
    
2.  将C:\ProgramData下的CellVue文件夹复制一份，备份
    
    ![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jD79QGDO01/img/09de1c47-5f78-472f-b4ee-61aa0376ebe1.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_1149,h_315/ignore-error,1)
    
3.  双击iso格式的升级包，会打开虚拟驱动器
    
    ![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jD79QGDO01/img/ea12d69b-847e-4860-b1f3-c6c8a1b5c24e.png)
    
4.  双击CellVue.xxx.Installer.exe进行安装，取消勾选开机自动启动，点击安装
    
    ![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jD79QGDO01/img/a7d64bee-01a0-472f-8164-a215db12c855.png?x-oss-process=image/crop,x_0,y_0,w_1266,h_570/ignore-error,1)![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jD79QGDO01/img/64224cb9-8d0b-425b-a4ab-5be90bca31a5.png)
    
5.  等待安装完成后，点击右上角“x”号，关闭安装窗口。升级完成，在电脑桌面上打开软件即是新版本的软件![image.png](https://alidocs.oss-cn-zhangjiakou.aliyuncs.com/res/2M9qP5jD79QGDO01/img/3a0da229-53b5-4dc9-ad63-4dc05b963695.png)
